    Connect to someone

    One person creates an invite and sends the code to the other (any messenger, email, …). The other person accepts it and sends a reply code back. The chat itself never goes through a server.

      P2P Chat

      Chat directly.
      No server.
      No sign‑up.

      Messages go straight from your browser to your friends' browsers, encrypted, so there's nobody in between who could store or read them.

      1. Your account lives on this device.It's a key pair created right here in your browser, without an email address or phone number.
      2. Connect by swapping a code.Send an invite code through any messenger. Your friend sends a reply code back. Once per person is enough.
      3. Chat while you're both online.Messages travel directly between you. Anything written while someone is away is sent when you're both online again.

      Open P2P Chat over a secure connection

      Your account uses your browser's built-in cryptography, which browsers only allow on https:// addresses, on localhost and for files opened from disk.

      Use martin-thoma.com/chat-app, open index.html directly, or run make run, which serves the app over HTTPS.

      P2P Chat is open in another tab

      Only one tab can be connected at a time.
